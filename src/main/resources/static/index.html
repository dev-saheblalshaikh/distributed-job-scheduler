What am I looking at?

  • SKIP LOCKED claiming: workers take different pending rows without waiting on rows another worker already picked.
  • Retries and backoff: a failed job waits longer between attempts, giving temporary problems time to clear.
  • Lease recovery: a heartbeat keeps a slow job owned; if a worker disappears, its expired lease lets another worker recover it.
